# Amino Acid Energy Filtering

This repository provides a simple yet powerful script for **filtering amino acids based on their energy values**.  
The main objective is to identify and separate amino acids with **negative energy contributions**, which are often of higher interest in molecular simulations and computational chemistry.

---

##  Motivation
In computational biochemistry, it is crucial to analyze amino acids not only by their structural properties but also by their **energetic stability**.  
This tool offers a straightforward approach to:
- Parse amino acid energy values  
- Extract those with **negative energies**  
- Generate a clean subset for further analysis (e.g., binding studies, structural stability checks)

---

##  Features
- Input: list of amino acids with associated energy values  
- Output: subset of amino acids with negative energy  
- Lightweight and fast  
- Easy to integrate into molecular dynamics (MD) post-processing pipelines.
